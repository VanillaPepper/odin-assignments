Masala Chai

Description

Chai is refreshing drink meant to calm you down ,where I come from people drink it every day in the morning

The recipe will only take 10 min to complete and serves one person

Ingredients

  • 1 cup water
  • 1 ½ teaspoons sugar
  • 1 whole cardamom pod
  • 1 whole clove
  • 2 black peppercorns
  • 3 teaspoons black tea leaves
  • ½ cup warm milk

Steps

  1. Combine the water and sugar in a small saucepan and boil
  2. Combine the water and sugar in a small saucepan
  3. Remove from heat, and let the mixture steep for 2 to 3 minutes
  4. Strain into a cup, and fill cup the rest of the way with milk
  5. Enjoy !
